# Email Schedules

* [Email Schedules](overview.md) - APIs for creating, updating, triggering, and deleting email schedules, and retrieving schedule details.

# Concepts

* [Change Email Schedule Status](change-email-schedule-status.md) - Activates or deactivates a schedule without deleting it.
* [Create Email Schedule](create-email-schedule.md) - Creates a new recurring email schedule in the workspace and returns its ID.
* [Delete Email Schedule](delete-email-schedule.md) - Permanently deletes an email schedule.
* [Get Email Schedules](get-email-schedules.md) - Returns every email schedule defined in the workspace, with its name, run period, enabled state, creator, and the views it delivers.
* [Trigger Email Schedule](trigger-email-schedule.md) - Runs a schedule immediately — the "send now" action.
* [Update Email Schedule](update-email-schedule.md) - Updates an existing schedule's name, run period, recipients, or email content.
