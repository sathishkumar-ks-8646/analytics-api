# Email Schedules

* [Email Schedules](overview.md) - APIs for creating, updating, triggering, and deleting email schedules, and retrieving schedule details.

# Concepts

* [Change Email Schedule Status](change-email-schedule-status.md) - Modify the status of the specified email schedule.
* [Create Email Schedule](create-email-schedule.md) - Create an email schedule in the specified workspace.
* [Delete Email Schedule](delete-email-schedule.md) - Delete the specified email schedule in the workspace.
* [Get Email Schedules](get-email-schedules.md) - Returns the list of email schedules available in the specified workspace.
* [Trigger Email Schedule](trigger-email-schedule.md) - Trigger the specified email schedule instantly, without waiting for its configured run time.
* [Update Email Schedule](update-email-schedule.md) - Update the configurations of the specified email schedule in the workspace.
