# Schedules & Alerts

* [Schedules & Alerts](overview.md) - API for Schedules & Alerts in Zoho Analytics — covering creation, management, and triggering of email schedules, and the full lifecycle of data alerts on a view.

# Subdirectories

* [Email Schedules](email-schedules/index.md) - APIs for creating, updating, triggering, and deleting email schedules, and retrieving schedule details.
* [Data Alerts](data-alerts/index.md) - APIs for creating, listing, updating, running and deleting data alerts on a view, changing their status and reading their event history.
