Exporting data

Synchronous export and the asynchronous job flow, including polling and expiry.

Two modes. Synchronous returns the file on the same call; asynchronous creates a job you poll and then download.

ModeCeilingUse when
Synchronous100 MBThe resource is not one of the restricted ones below.
Asynchronous jobNo practical ceilingRequired for any restricted resource, and for anything over the synchronous ceiling.

The choice is not about convenience. Export Data API is restricted for the resources below; for these you must use the asynchronous export, and the synchronous call rejects them with 8133:

  • Tables having more than one million rows.
  • Tables and Views from live connect workspaces.
  • Dashboard and Query Table view types.

Note

Use the returned downloadUrl

It is fully qualified and already points at the correct data center. Do not assemble download URLs by hand — they differ by region.

Warning

Jobs and files expire one hour after the job completes

Both the job record and the generated file are retained for one hour from the moment the job reports complete — not from when it was created. expiryTime carries the exact deadline. Download as soon as the job completes, or create a new job; nothing is recoverable afterwards.

Limit

Five concurrent export jobs per organization

A sixth is rejected with error 8132. Poll and drain the queue rather than creating jobs in a loop.

Polling#

Poll at a sensible interval — a few seconds for small exports, tens of seconds for large ones. jobStatus moves through JOB NOT STARTED, JOB IN PROGRESS, then JOB COMPLETED or JOB FAILED. Treat JOB FAILED as terminal; the response carries the reason.
